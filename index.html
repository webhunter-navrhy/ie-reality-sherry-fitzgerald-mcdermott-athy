About the property

Ballintubbert is a charming 4 Bedroom Bungalow, located along the Ballintubbert Road just off the N80 in a Peaceful Rural Location Between Stradbally & Athy Town. Sitting on its own spacious site of C. 0.49 acres / 0.20 hectares and surrounded by panoramic views of the countryside. Comprising C. 117 sq. m of internal living space.Nestled on a generous plot of land, this bungalow presents a wonderful opportunity for those looking to renovate and make it their own.While the property would benefit from some upgrades and renovation, it offers great potential for the right buyer. This is a fantastic opportunity to create a cosey countryside home in a tranquil location.Accommodation includes: Sitting Room, Dining Room, Kitchen, Guest W.C., 4 Bedrooms and Bathroom. Primary & secondary schools are within close proximity.Viewing is strictly by appointment with sole selling agent Adrian Harley of Sherry FitzGerald McDermott.To see current offers or place an offer on this property, register for your mySherryFitz account at SherryFitz.ie
